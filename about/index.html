About Us

We are driven by a passion for design and construction with a commitment to excellence.

“Engineered with Passion,
Inspired by Possibilities”

Welcome to RCI, where engineering meets innovation and creativity fuels every design. Founded with a vision to revolutionize the world of MEP engineering, we’ve evolved into a dynamic team of experts who are dedicated to crafting solutions that shape the future of built environments.

Our Journey

What began as a passion for innovative engineering has grown into a journey of excellence. Our founders envisioned a firm that not only embraces cutting-edge technology but also places people and sustainability at the core of every project. Today, that vision guides us as we engineer spaces that are efficient, comfortable, and environmentally responsible.

Our Vision

To be the foremost customer-centric engineering firm, delivering excellence in every interaction and being the trusted partner where clients confidently place all their project needs, knowing we deliver optimal and tailored solutions for their buildings.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to providing our clients with expertly crafted designs, leveraging our knowledge and effective communication to identify the most suitable engineering technologies within project budgets and timelines. We consistently deliver applicable and innovative solutions that exceed our clients’ expectations.

Our Values

At RCI Engineering, Inc., our identity is woven into the fabric of our five core values. These values are not just words; they are the guiding principles that define who we are and shape the essence of our work.

As the pillars of our organization, these values serve as the compass that directs our efforts, ensuring that every project reflects our commitment to excellence, innovation, and client satisfaction.

We embrace these values daily, using them as a benchmark to uphold the highest standards in our industry.

Continuous Learning

Deliver designs that not only meet code compliance but also incorporate the latest advancements and the most applicable equipment, ensuring our solutions are at the forefront of industry knowledge.

Punctual Execution

Meticulously adhering to deadlines and delivering designs that seamlessly align with construction requirements, ensuring that project timelines remain on track without unnecessary delays.

Client Centricity

We prioritize our clients by actively listening to their needs, allowing us to adapt projects’ basis of design, deadlines, and budgets to align with their unique requirements and provide an experience that goes beyond expectations.

Uncompromising Quality

We stand firm to not compromising public safety or our quality in the face of time constraints, external pressures, unrealistic expectations, company growth, or any other factors.

Effective Communication

Our commitment to transparent and responsive communication ensures that your vision is understood, your concerns are addressed, and our collaboration is built on a foundation of bringing your projects to successful fruition.
