Open Positions

We’re always on the lookout for exceptional talent to help us innovate, collaborate, and drive excellence. If you’re passionate about engineering, innovation, and are looking for an opportunity to make a meaningful impact, explore our current job openings below. Your journey to join a team committed to engineering brilliance begins here.

Project Manager

Responsible for overseeing the planning, execution, and successful completion of MEP engineering projects design. Their tasks include project coordination, stakeholder communication, budget management, timeline adherence, and ensuring that all aspects of the project meet rigorous quality standards.

Project Coordinator

Plays a crucial role in our MEP engineering projects, serving as the organizational linchpin. Their responsibilities encompass project scheduling, team coordination, document management, and communication with clients and stakeholders. They work closely with the Project Manager to ensure smooth project flow, meeting timelines, and quality standards, ultimately contributing to the successful delivery of projects.

HVAC Engineer / Designer

Responsible for creating innovative, efficient, and sustainable HVAC systems. Their tasks involve system design, calculations, layout planning, and collaboration with the team to ensure seamless integration of mechanical solutions into projects.

Electrical Engineer / Designer

Responsible for developing cutting-edge electrical systems that power our projects. Their tasks encompass power distribution, lighting design, renewable energy integration, and the implementation of intelligent control systems.

Plumbing Engineer / Designer

Responsible for creating efficient and sustainable plumbing systems. Their tasks include designing water supply, sanitary, and stormwater systems, along with implementing rainwater harvesting and water-efficient solutions.

Fire Protection Engineer / Designer

Responsible of designing and implementing fire protection systems, including fire sprinklers and fire alarms. Their tasks involve system design, code compliance, and ensuring rapid response to potential threats.
