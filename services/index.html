Our Services

We offer a comprehensive range of MEP design services tailored to meet the needs of each project. Our dedicated team of experts combines technical proficiency with creative problem-solving to deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

“Efficient Climate Solutions, Engineered for Comfort”

Mechanical

RCI specializes in designing cutting-edge Mechanical HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning) systems that create comfortable and energy-efficient indoor environments. From maintaining consistent temperatures to enhancing indoor air quality, our Mechanical HVAC services are designed to create spaces where occupants thrive. With a keen eye on innovation and sustainability, we’re ready to engineer the perfect climate solution for your project.

HVAC System Design

From concept to implementation, we design HVAC systems tailored to your project’s specific needs, ensuring optimal thermal comfort year-round.

Energy Efficiency

Our experts incorporate advanced technologies to create energy-efficient HVAC designs that reduce operational costs and minimize environmental impact.

Indoor Air Quality

We prioritize indoor air quality by designing ventilation systems that ensure a continuous supply of fresh air, promoting a healthier and more productive environment.

HVAC Retrofitting

Upgrade your existing HVAC systems for enhanced performance, energy savings, and compliance with modern efficiency standards.

Climate Control Solutions

Whether it’s a commercial building, a residential complex, or an industrial facility, we engineer climate control systems that meet the unique demands of each space.

Power Distribution Design

Lighting Design

Renewable Energy

Emergency Power Systems

Control Systems

“Elevating Performance, Ensuring Excellence”

Commissioning

We go beyond design and construction—we ensure that your MEP systems perform at their best through our specialized MEP commissioning services. MEP commissioning is the bridge that connects design and operation, guaranteeing that systems function optimally and deliver the expected performance. Our commissioning process enhances your project’s value, minimizes operational risks, and ensures long-term satisfaction for occupants.

Design Review

We begin by closely reviewing your MEP designs to identify potential issues and ensure alignment with project goals.

Systems Verification

Our experts thoroughly inspect and verify the installation of MEP systems to confirm they match design intent.

Funtional Testing

We rigorously test MEP systems to verify that they perform as intended, ensuring proper integration and functionality.

Performance Optimization

Our team fine-tunes systems for maximum efficiency and occupant comfort, utilizing data-driven insights.

Documentation

We provide comprehensive documentation of commissioning activities, serving as a valuable resource for future maintenance.

40/25 Years Recertification

We provide city and county required inspection to existing buildings to guarantee the safety of its occupants.
