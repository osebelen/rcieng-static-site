Who We Are

RCI is a Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing, and Fire Protection Consulting Engineering firm located in South Florida, founded in 2005.

Our Professional Engineers (PEs) hold licenses in multiple states, yet we primarily focus our MEP service provision on the vibrant South Florida market through our specialized studios: High-end Residential, Commercial, and Multifamily.

We aim to seamlessly integrate with clients, making them feel as if they have a trusted in-house MEP department for a collaborative and streamlined design experience.

Why RCI

Communication: At RCI, communication is a system. From onboarding questionnaires to structured weekly updates, we keep clients informed and aligned every step of the way.

Proven Track Record: Since 2005, we’ve delivered 100+ projects annually trusted by leading architects and developers across South Florida with a 9.7/10 satisfaction rating based on our satisfaction surveys Responsiveness When It Matters Most: In both design and construction, timing is critical. We respond quickly—often within 48 hours—to keep momentum and avoid costly delays.

Curated, Collaborative Solutions: We take a boutique, collaborative approach—crafting tailored MEP solutions that honor design intent, technical precision, and project realities.

We take pride in our extensive portfolio of successfully completed projects.
